Pet Animator

Pick a set, a pet and an animation (Idle, Play, Dance). Pose the pet on the stage key by key; the firmware blends between keys with the same easing. Save writes the set's moves.json; sh tools/emu/build_pm.sh then picks it up.

Tools (Body)

1 PoseDrag a hand or a foot and its arm or leg follows; drag the small ring past its tip to turn it in place without moving it. Drag the head to move it off its neck; its ring, above it, turns it. The same for a drum or xylophone: drag to move, ring to turn. The body's ring, just off its left edge, turns the body alone: hands and feet stay where they are, the head and instrument keep their bearing, the tail goes with the body. The Turn tool on the body turns everything with it. Drag a forearm or a shin and its elbow or knee swings towards the cursor while the hand or foot stays put (Flip bend in the Selected part section bends it the other way). Drag the body (or its orange square, or Alt+drag anywhere) and the hands and feet stay where they are. Planted feet: drag the foot, the knee follows. Other parts turn.
2 TurnEvery part turns about its own joint.
3 Move petSlide the whole pet, planted feet and all.
4 Slide partSlide one part off its joint (the head forward, down into the shoulders); what hangs from it goes along.
