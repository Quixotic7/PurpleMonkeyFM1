## PurpleMonkey 0.10

The first public release: a firmware that turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a first instrument for a small child. Press
any key and something nice happens. No menus, nothing to save, nothing to break.

### What is in it

- **SYNTH**: every key is a note on a five-note scale over three octaves, so no key is a wrong note. The pet sings
  a tapped note back a bar later, held keys grow a phrase, three quick keys start a run.
- **DRUMS**: every key is a drum (nine sounds, three times over). A held key repeats in its sound's own rhythm.
- **BEAT**: a two-bar backing pattern per pet, with a bass and a round of four chords under SYNTH.
- **TUNE**: any key plays the next note of a nursery tune; twelve tunes, three a pet in each world.
- **TALK**: each key says its letter, A to Z, and shows it big; the last key says "yay". The voice is linear
  predictive speech in the manner of the talking toys of 1978: 33 words in about 7 KB, no recording.
- **Four friends**: Monkey, Cat, Dog and Llama, each with its own sound, drum kit, beat, chords and tunes. A new
  pet says its name.
- **Sixteen sounds** (four a pet) on FM6, a six-operator FM engine, and **six worlds** for the pet to stand in:
  night, sea, balloons, space, meadow, snow.
- **Four knobs** that cannot break the sound: TONE, WOBBLE, SPACE, LENGTH in SYNTH; SPEED, BUSY, BOUNCE, SQUISH in
  DRUMS. HOME puts them back.
- MIDI in (USB and TRS): a keyboard plays the pet's sound, channel 10 its kit. USB audio out to a computer.
- A browser emulator (the same firmware compiled to WebAssembly) and a browser installer.

### Good to know

- This is a first beta. Installing firmware is at your own risk; read "Recovery" on the site first.
- Update mode is OCT− + OCT+ + HOME held for 5 seconds (three buttons, so a small hand does not find it).
- PurpleMonkey stores nothing. Installing it over Felucca or ChoralRoot replaces what they stored: the installer
  saves a backup file first, to restore after you go back.
- On a computer the FM-1 still shows ChoralRoot's USB names.
