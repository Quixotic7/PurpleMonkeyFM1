## PurpleMonkey 0.11

A small release of fixes after 0.10.

### Fixed

- **TALK: the big letter now always shows.** About half the time (depending on the millisecond a key went down)
  the letter was cleared in the moment it appeared, so the key spoke but showed nothing.
- **The Cat's right ear.** Its resting picture was drawn a quarter turn off the other three, and the poses turned
  it to compensate, so the ear lay over sideways whenever the face changed (singing a note, happy with keys held,
  surprised, sleepy) and while dancing. The picture is now drawn the same way as the others and each face places
  its ears itself.

### For people animating the pets

- The Face editor of the pet animator (`tools/rig_editor/`) has an x and a y for each ear in every face key, next
  to its turn, and the firmware applies them: an ear's pictures were drawn one by one and do not all sit alike.

Installing over 0.10 needs nothing special: PurpleMonkey stores nothing.
